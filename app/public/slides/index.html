What a new Builder hits

Unexplained variants

Three Indopak scripts, seven transliterations, eight downloads of one translation. Nothing says how they differ.

One sentence per variant on the catalog page.

Undocumented shapes

A tafsir entry is sometimes a string pointing at another key. Segments are word-level milliseconds. Every ayah ends in a glyph only one font renders.

A schema and gotchas section per resource type.

Licensing silence

No resource page shows a licence. The FAQ points to per-resource terms that do not exist.

A licence field in the catalog, on the page and in the export.

Guidance in the wrong place

"Self-host the audio" lives in a tutorial, not next to the audio URL. Sign-in is required and unannounced.

Put the warning where the URL is. Say sign-in up front.
